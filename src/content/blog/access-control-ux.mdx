## The gap

Most physical access control software was designed by engineers, for integrators, not for the operators who use it daily. The result is interfaces that expose system architecture rather than support user tasks.

## Why it persists

The procurement chain in physical security runs through system integrators, not end users. The buyer and the user are rarely the same person. This creates a market dynamic where usability isn't a purchasing criterion — compatibility and certification are.

## What good looks like

A handful of newer entrants are proving that security-grade reliability and considered UX aren't mutually exclusive. The pattern in every case: they started from operator workflows, not from the data model.
