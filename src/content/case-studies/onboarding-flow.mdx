## Problem

Physical access operators at mid-size facilities were abandoning the credential provisioning flow at a high rate during the initial setup phase. Support tickets spiked in the first 72 hours post-deployment.

## Process

Conducted contextual inquiry sessions with five operators at three separate sites. Mapped the existing 14-step provisioning flow against mental models from interviews. Identified three categories of friction: terminology mismatches, unclear confirmation states, and premature demands for information operators didn't have at that stage.

## Outcome

Redesigned flow reduced provisioning time by 40% in usability testing. Support ticket volume for first-login issues dropped significantly in the first month post-launch.
